Frequently Asked Questions

Troubleshooting, maintenance and import support.

IMPA is a product coding system, not a certification. We match every part we supply to its IMPA catalogue code (ship storage batteries fall under Class 17), and provide the code reference alongside the documentation your surveyor needs.

We supply a compliance pack with every lithium shipment — UN38.3 test summaries and MSDS documents — and pack cells to IATA (air) and IMDG (ocean) dangerous-goods rules, so clearance runs smoothly at the destination port.

Many of our cells come in standard case sizes that fit standard racking. Share your current model and charging system before swapping, so we can confirm the chemistry, voltage and terminals are a correct match.
