Who We Are

A Specialist Marine Battery Supplier.

MUSE MARINE sources and supplies industrial small batteries and GMDSS safety cells for chandlers, ship managers and fleet buyers. We are not a general electronics vendor — we focus on one narrow line and do it properly.

Why We Exist

The Right Part, Fast, Every Time.

At sea, a wrong or late battery means a survey failure or an unplanned stop. We built MUSE MARINE around a simple promise: when a chandler or ship manager sends a model or IMPA code, we return the exact-fit replacement — with the right documentation and a realistic delivery date.

Every cell we supply is matched to its IMPA code and GMDSS replacement cycle, and ships with UN38.3 test summaries and MSDS documents as standard, so customs clearance never holds up your delivery.
